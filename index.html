Designed for morning clarity

01

Show When Locked

Uses native Android Keyguard APIs to render directly above your lockscreen. The moment your display turns on, your diagram is ready.

02

Hold-to-Confirm Physics

When waking up half-asleep, single taps are easily misclicked. Holding the card for 650ms smoothly fills it with green before locking the checkmark.

03

Zero Distractions

Replaces Samsung lockscreen clock, badges, and notification alerts with a calm paper-like canvas. Swipe up anytime to proceed to your regular PIN.

Open Source & Attribution

The concept diagram was generated with Gemini, inspired by the minimalist hand-drawn design language of Excalidraw, and brought to life natively with Jetpack Compose.
